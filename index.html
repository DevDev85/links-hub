About Develop Device

Develop Device is the studio of Jaroslav Holub, a producer and sound designer building production tools for modern rock and metal since 2012. The catalogue covers Superior Drummer 3, EZdrummer 3, GetGood Drums and MODO Drum presets, amp tones for Fractal Axe-Fx, Neural DSP Quad Cortex, Kemper, Line 6 Helix and NAM, Serum 2 and Vital synth presets, drum MIDI packs, DAW templates and plugins such as HumBeat, ShredSync and OBSIDIAN.

Every product is an instant download. Artists including Jeff Loomis, Tim Lambesis, Chris Baseford and Ahrue Luster use Develop Device presets and templates, and custom mixing, mastering and preset work is handled personally in the studio.
