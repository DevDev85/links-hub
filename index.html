About Develop Device

Develop Device is the one-man studio of Jaroslav Holub, producer and sound designer. I have been building production tools for modern rock and metal since 2012. The catalogue covers Superior Drummer 3, EZdrummer 3, GetGood Drums and MODO Drum presets, amp tones for Fractal Axe-Fx, Neural DSP Quad Cortex, Kemper, Line 6 Helix and NAM, and synth presets for Serum 2, Vital, ArgWave 3, Massive XL, Hydrasynth, DeepMind 12 and Korg modwave and multi/poly.

There are also drum MIDI packs, DAW templates and my own plugins and apps such as HumBeat 3, DupTraX 3, ShredSync 3, OBSIDIAN 2 and ArgWave 3; 16 of them have a free 7-day trial. Every product is an instant download. Artists including Jeff Loomis, Tim Lambesis, Chris Baseford and Ahrue Luster use my presets and templates.
